Author

Pablo Adrián Zúñiga Guamán

I am a Master’s student in Statistics at the University of Campinas (UNICAMP), Brazil, working in probability theory under the supervision of Christophe Gallesco. I hold a BSc in Statistics from Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral (ESPOL), Ecuador.

My research interests include probability theory, stochastic processes, ergodic theory, dynamical systems, processes with infinite memory, and stochastic partial differential equations. My current work concerns the compatibility and uniqueness of probability laws in infinite-memory shift systems.

Current research

Hellinger Criteria for Uniqueness and Dynamic Uniqueness in Infinite-Memory Shift Systems

I study compatible measures for chains with complete connections and \(g\)-measures on standard Borel spaces. The project develops Hellinger-type criteria connecting dependence on the remote past with future absolute continuity, ergodic rigidity, and asymptotic loss of memory.
